# Unit Cost Methodology

Unit-cost metrics are descriptive synthetic figures. They are not audited finance results and are not savings
recommendations.

Included recurring components are configured as lease, maintenance, utility, security, cleaning and business rates.
Planned capital cost, exit cost and relocation cost are excluded from recurring operating cost. Building costs are
allocated across available, booked and occupied room-hour denominators and completed contacts.

Finance records requiring manual review are excluded from the calculation. The finance defect `FIN-00002` remains
visible in exclusion evidence.
