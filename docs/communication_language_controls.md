# Communication Language Controls

Milestone 12 uses deterministic wording controls.

Required transformations:

- `saving` becomes `nominal recurring cost difference`;
- `recommended` becomes `strongest configured result, subject to constraints`;
- `optimal solution` becomes `lowest configured mathematical objective`;
- `releasable building` becomes `potentially releasable in the mathematical candidate`;
- `financially viable` becomes `nominally financially positive under configured assumptions`.

When resilience has failed, outputs retain:

```text
not realisable without mitigation
```

Unsupported phrases such as `board approved`, `must implement`, `clinically safe`, `validated in practice` and `stakeholders agreed` are rejected unless used in an explicit negation or boundary statement.
