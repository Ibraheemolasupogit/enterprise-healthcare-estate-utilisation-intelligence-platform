# Financial Cash Flows

Annual cash flows are produced for the configured five-year horizon. Each row records baseline recurring cost, case recurring cost, gross recurring cost difference, transition costs, mitigation costs, implementation costs, net annual financial effect, discount factor, discounted cash flow and cumulative cash flow.

Benefits are phased through the configured ramp: year 1 partial realisation, year 2 higher realisation and year 3 onward full configured realisation. Cost escalation and demand-growth assumptions are applied deterministically.

Cash-flow outputs preserve nominal effects separately from discounted and risk-adjusted effects.
