# Financial Break-Even

Break-even evidence includes the maximum transition cost before NPV becomes zero, the minimum annual effect required for payback within five years and the maximum mitigation cost before annual financial effect becomes negative.

The calculations use deterministic formulas and configured assumptions. They are intended for planning challenge and sensitivity discussion, not as approval thresholds.

Break-even results remain dependent on operational resilience, implementation feasibility and the synthetic nature of the cost assumptions.
