# Stakeholder Map

| Stakeholder group | Interest | Concerns | Evidence required | Communication level | Influence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Clinical service leads | Protect service quality and suitable clinical space. | Loss of specialist rooms, unsafe moves, disruption. | Room suitability, activity, demand, clinical constraints. | Detailed clinical rationale. | High |
| Operational managers | Maintain flow, capacity and service continuity. | Unworkable schedules, resilience loss, transition burden. | Utilisation, peaks, contingency and operational constraints. | Practical operational summaries. | High |
| Booking teams | Understand room demand and booking behaviour. | Data quality, double-booking, local exceptions. | Booking rules, cancellations, attendance links. | Process-level detail. | Medium |
| Estates and facilities | Manage estate condition, capacity and compliance. | Physical feasibility, backlog, maintenance constraints. | Building, room, condition, accessibility and cost evidence. | Technical estates detail. | High |
| Workforce planning | Align space options with staff availability. | Workforce shortages, rota mismatch, specialist dependency. | Staffing model, availability and service coverage evidence. | Planning assumptions and risks. | Medium |
| Finance | Understand costs, benefits and uncertainty. | Unsupported savings claims, hidden transition costs. | Cost categories, sensitivity and confidence levels. | Financial summary with traceable assumptions. | High |
| Transformation | Shape feasible change options. | Benefits not realised, stakeholder resistance. | Scenario comparison, risk and implementation dependencies. | Option appraisal narrative. | Medium |
| Information governance | Protect data and lawful processing. | Real patient data exposure, weak controls. | Data boundary, lineage and access controls. | Assurance-focused evidence. | High |
| Executive decision-makers | Decide whether options proceed through governance. | Strategic risk, reputational impact, incomplete evidence. | Balanced scorecard, uncertainties and recommendation rationale. | Concise decision briefing. | Very high |
| Patient and accessibility interests | Protect equitable access and continuity. | Travel burden, inaccessible rooms, service displacement. | Accessibility, location and service impact evidence. | Clear impact summaries. | High |

Milestone 12 uses stakeholder groups only. It does not name real individuals or claim real stakeholder agreement.
