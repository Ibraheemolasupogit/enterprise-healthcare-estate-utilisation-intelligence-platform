# Dashboard Pages

Milestone 11 includes twelve local Streamlit pages.

1. Executive Overview: estate utilisation, operational resilience and financial implications.
2. Estate Portfolio: sites, buildings, ownership, cost, rooms, utilisation and lease fields.
3. Room Utilisation: booked, actual, attendance and effective utilisation with protected-capacity caveats.
4. Clinical Activity: aggregated synthetic contacts, completion, DNA, cancellation and delivery mix.
5. Workforce: planned and available FTE, absence, vacancy, capacity and simulation bottlenecks.
6. Data Quality: scores, issues, manual-review queue, intentional defects, reconciliation and exclusions.
7. Forecasting: 24-month historical series, selected models, accuracy and forecast intervals.
8. Scenario Comparison: four heuristic scenarios without implementation selection.
9. Optimisation: mathematical allocations, objective components, constraints and diagnostics.
10. Simulation Resilience: failed case/experiment threshold evidence and workforce bottlenecks.
11. Financial Impact: nominal and risk-adjusted effects, payback, sensitivity and break-even evidence.
12. Evidence and Limitations: lineage, evidence counts, assumptions and milestone boundaries.
13. Communication and Decision Record: read-only communication products, objections, revisions, claims and decision status.

Every page shows the global synthetic-data notice, current run identifiers, reference period and limitations pointer.
