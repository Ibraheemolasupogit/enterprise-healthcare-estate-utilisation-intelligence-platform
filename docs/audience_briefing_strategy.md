# Audience Briefing Strategy

Executive leaders receive concise decision context, option comparison, resilience findings, financial implications, conditions precedent and governance choices.

Clinical and operational leaders receive service continuity, capacity, waiting, completion, workforce and mitigation-test evidence. Financial value is not the lead message for this audience.

Finance stakeholders receive baseline recurring cost, transition and mitigation cost, cash-flow, NPV, payback, sensitivity, break-even and risk-adjusted realisability evidence.

Estates and facilities stakeholders receive building, room, lease, ownership, condition, accessibility, protected-capacity and transition-burden evidence. Potential release language is caveated as mathematical candidate evidence only.

Technical reviewers receive lineage, checksums, quality controls, formulae, forecast validation, scenario rules, optimisation status, simulation design, sensitivity and limitations.
