# Scenario Cost Methodology

Scenario cost evidence uses recurring operating-cost components already present in Milestone 5 unit-cost evidence.
It reports baseline recurring cost, scenario recurring cost and descriptive recurring cost difference.

Transition, planned capital, exit and relocation exposures are reported separately as indicative exposure values.
The engine does not calculate NPV, payback, audited savings or benefit realisation.

Milestone 8 objective components use configured synthetic coefficients. They remain allocation objective terms, not
audited financial benefits or final business-case values.
## Milestone 10 Handoff

Scenario cost evidence is consumed conservatively by Milestone 10. Heuristic room actions do not remove fixed building-level recurring costs unless financial-release conditions are independently satisfied.
