# Decision Criteria

Future recommendations should be assessed against:

- service continuity;
- patient access;
- forecast capacity;
- specialist room compatibility;
- workforce feasibility;
- operational resilience;
- accessibility;
- cost;
- transition burden;
- evidence quality;
- uncertainty;
- reversibility.

Operational feasibility takes precedence over nominal savings. A lower-cost option that undermines service continuity,
patient access, specialist capacity or workforce feasibility should not be treated as preferred without explicit
governance review.

Milestone 7 applies these criteria as scenario constraints and comparison dimensions only. The comparison score is a
descriptive appraisal signal, not a recommendation, approval, optimisation result, or financial benefits case.

Milestone 8 applies these criteria as mathematical optimisation constraints and objective components. The lowest
configured objective is not an operational approval or implementation recommendation.

Milestone 9 applies operational resilience thresholds to simulated allocation cases. Threshold breaches are evidence
for review, not final accept/reject decisions.
## Financial Decision-Support Criteria

Financial outputs separate nominal, discounted and risk-adjusted effects. A positive nominal NPV is not sufficient for deliverability where operational simulation thresholds failed. Such cases remain `not_realisable_without_mitigation` until operational remediation is evidenced.

## Dashboard Interpretation Criteria

Milestone 11 surfaces these criteria but does not convert them into approval. The dashboard keeps failed simulation resilience and financial non-realisability visible wherever scenario, optimisation or financial evidence is explored.

## Communication Criteria

Milestone 12 translates the criteria into audience-specific products and a decision record. It does not select a final option. Governance choices remain neutral and approval status remains `not_approved`.

Milestone 13 validates that engineering release readiness does not change the decision status or approval status.
