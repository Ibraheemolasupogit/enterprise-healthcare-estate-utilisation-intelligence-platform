# Optimisation Infeasibility

Infeasibility diagnostics identify service-period demand without enough eligible capacity, candidate blocks, room-type
shortfalls, specialist-equipment blocks, workforce restrictions, accessibility restrictions, co-location conflicts,
confidentiality conflicts and protected-capacity conflicts.

The framework does not fabricate an irreducible infeasible set. It records deterministic diagnostic evidence that can
be reviewed by clinical, operational, estates, workforce, accessibility and finance stakeholders.

An infeasible mathematical case is a valid analytical result. It is not an execution failure unless the solver or data
contract fails.

Milestone 9 may identify operational threshold breaches even when a Milestone 8 mathematical case is feasible.
