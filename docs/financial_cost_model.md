# Financial Cost Model

Recurring estate costs are separated into fixed, semi-variable and variable components. Fixed costs remain until a building satisfies the configured financial-release conditions. Room deactivation alone does not remove lease cost, business rates, fixed security, fixed maintenance or fixed cleaning costs.

Transition costs are recorded separately from recurring operating costs. They include lease-exit, relocation, refurbishment, technology enablement, transition staffing, change management, temporary dual running and contingency costs. Mitigation costs are linked to failed simulation thresholds and are reported separately from nominal recurring cost differences.

The model excludes planned capital, exit and relocation fields from recurring operating cost calculations to avoid double-counting.
