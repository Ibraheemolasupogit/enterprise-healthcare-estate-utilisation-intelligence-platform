# Simulation Resources

The simulation models room resources, service queues and workforce contact capacity. Room resources respect active room
status, opening and closing times, protected flags and specialist equipment indicators from curated and evidence tables.

Workforce capacity is derived from forecast session capacity by service where available, with configured absence
multipliers in workforce stress experiments. The model does not assume cross-site transfer unless upstream allocation
evidence already places activity at that site.

Specialist-room disruption reduces available specialist-room time in the configured experiment.
