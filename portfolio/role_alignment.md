# Role Alignment

The project aligns to analytics engineering, data product, and healthcare intelligence roles.

## Analytics Engineering

It shows reproducible pipelines, typed Python modules, SQL schemas, tests, contract validation, and evidence exports.

## Healthcare Intelligence

It models estate utilisation, room capacity, clinical demand, workforce constraints, operational resilience, and stakeholder governance using synthetic evidence.

## Product and Stakeholder Delivery

It includes dashboard pages, audience-specific communication outputs, decision records, limitations, runbooks, and handover materials.

The strongest role signal is the balance between delivery and judgement: the platform produces a substantial synthetic evidence base while preserving `awaiting_governance_decision` and `not_approved`.
